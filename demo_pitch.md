# VantageTime — demo pitch

Every indie film starts the same way: a script, a deadline, and a production coordinator drowning in spreadsheets — breaking down scenes by hand, guessing at a shoot schedule, chasing a dozen actors for their availability by text, then building a call sheet at midnight before the shoot.

VantageTime automates that whole chain. Upload a screenplay PDF, and a pipeline of Google ADK agents takes it from there: one agent breaks the script into scenes, cast, and locations; a team of research agents pull real permit and weather data for each location via live web search; a scheduling agent groups scenes into shoot days and checks its own work with deterministic Python — real page counts, real calendar dates, real weather forecasts, never guessed; and a call sheet generator turns all of it into a professional, per-day call sheet with cast calls, safety notes, and production contacts.

Cast and crew get a magic link to submit real availability, so the schedule is built around who can actually show up — not who the AI assumed could.

It's the production coordinator indie filmmakers can't afford to hire, minus the hallucinated call times.
